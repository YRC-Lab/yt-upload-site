yt-upload

yt-upload is a personal command-line tool used by one individual to upload their own video recordings to their own YouTube channel, attach subtitle tracks, add the videos to the matching playlists, and write chapter markers into the video descriptions.

It is not offered to the public. It runs only on the developer's own computer and is authorized only by the developer's own Google account.

YouTube access used

The tool requests the YouTube scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl to upload videos, upload caption tracks, update video descriptions and settings, and add videos to playlists on the developer's own channel.

See the Privacy Policy.
